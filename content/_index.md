Member of Technical Staff at Rime. I am also an MSc student in Computer Science at ETH Zurich, majoring in Machine Intelligence, where I work on multimodal AI research at the DISCO group (Prof. Wattenhofer), focused on speech generation, large-scale multilingual audio data, and post-training. My recent work includes WorldSpeech, the largest publicly available human-transcribed multilingual speech corpus (65k hours, 80+ languages). I also maintain a public notebook of technical notes from my research.
